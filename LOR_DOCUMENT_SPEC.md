# Zinternora — Letter of Recommendation

The completion document set now includes:

1. Internship Offer Letter — available after payment approval.
2. Certificate of Completion — available after all four weeks are completed.
3. Letter of Recommendation (LOR) — available after successful completion.

The LOR is personalized from the student's approved profile:
- Student name
- Internship domain
- Internship duration (4 weeks / 1 month)
- Completion date
- Unique LOR ID

Suggested LOR wording:

TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN

This letter is to recommend [STUDENT NAME], who successfully completed the
4-week [DOMAIN] Internship Program at Zinternora.

During the internship, [STUDENT NAME] completed assigned weekly tasks,
practical activities, submissions, and the final project through the Zinternora
internship dashboard. The program provided hands-on exposure to practical
workflows and project-based learning in [DOMAIN].

We appreciate [STUDENT NAME]'s participation, commitment to completing the
assigned work, and willingness to learn throughout the program.

We wish [STUDENT NAME] continued success in future academic and professional
endeavours.

Sincerely,

Faree Chaudhry
Founder & CEO
Zinternora
