# Document Signatories

All three official documents (Offer Letter, Certificate, Letter of Recommendation) show both signatories:

- Faree Chaudhry — Founder & CEO
- Ria Chaudhry — HR Manager

Their handwritten signatures come from the images they supplied, stored in `public/signatures/`
(`faree-chaudhry.png`, `ria-chaudhry.png`) and referenced in `src/lib/brand.ts`.
To replace one, overwrite the PNG (transparent background). To leave a line blank for a wet signature, set `signatureImage` to `null`.
Zinternora never draws, types or generates a signature.
