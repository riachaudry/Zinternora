# Zinternora Submission Workflow

- Each live task has a real unlock date and deadline.
- Deadline is the Sunday cutoff for that task's week, at 11:59 PM.
- Student submits file/link/text from the dashboard.
- On successful submission, the next task becomes available automatically.
- After the final task of a week is submitted, the next week opens automatically.
- Overdue student submissions are blocked server-side.
- Admin can reopen an overdue/revision task; reopening sets the deadline to the current date and notifies the student.
- Admin review, scoring, feedback, revision and rejection remain available.
