# Zinternora — 1 Month Task Structure

The 16 launch domains each use a four-week roadmap.

Unlocking order:
1. Payment verified
2. Personalized offer letter becomes available
3. Week 1 Task 1 opens
4. Required tasks unlock sequentially after submission/approval
5. Completing all required Week 1 tasks unlocks Week 2 Task 1 automatically
6. The same sequence repeats for Week 3 and Week 4
7. Week 4 is the final project/completion week

The existing roadmap files contain field-specific practical tasks. The seed script publishes them to Supabase for all 16 domains.

- Web Development

- Mobile App Development
- Data Science & Analytics
